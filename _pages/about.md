## About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/), affiliated with the [NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/) and supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://junxianh.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. degree from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024.

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**, with particular interest in:
- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Developing verifiable reasoning data and training methodologies to enhance logical reasoning capabilities in large language models.
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Understanding and mitigating perceptual bottlenecks in multi-modal systems, particularly for complex reasoning tasks.
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Investigating the internal representations that govern factual consistency and developing theoretically-grounded techniques for more truthful and interpretable LLMs.

I actively collaborate with leading AI research labs and have industry experience at top AI companies.
