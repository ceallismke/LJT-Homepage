SynLogic is a framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale for training LLMs to perform logical reasoning. We generate diverse, formally verifiable reasoning chains using symbolic methods and show that models trained on this data significantly outperform existing approaches on logical reasoning benchmarks.
