This paper identifies a fundamental perception bottleneck in Vision-Language Models when processing charts and visual data. We show that the visual encoding stage fails to capture key structural elements of charts, leading to systematic hallucinations and reasoning errors in multi-modal reasoning tasks. Our analysis motivates better visual-LLM integration for chart reasoning.
