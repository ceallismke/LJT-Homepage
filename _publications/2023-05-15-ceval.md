C-Eval is a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models, consisting of 13,948 multiple-choice questions across 52 diverse subjects, covering different levels and difficulty ranges from middle school to advanced professional knowledge. This benchmark provides a standardized way to evaluate Chinese LLM capabilities.
