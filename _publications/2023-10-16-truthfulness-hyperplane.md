This paper introduces the universal truthfulness hyperplane, a single linear direction within LLM representations that distinguishes truthful from false answers across diverse factual claims. We show this hyperplane is remarkably consistent across languages, model scales, and fine-tuning methods, and demonstrate its use for unsupervised detection of hallucinations and factual inconsistencies.
